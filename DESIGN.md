## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Mutta Mystery Board"**

MuttaMeter's aesthetic is a blend of "Bakery x Comic x Investigation Board". It rejects the sterile, high-gloss minimalism of modern food-delivery apps in favor of a tactile, handcrafted, and chaotic feel. The visual language is "colorful soft brutalism"—thick playful borders, expressive badges, and cream paper textures that make the app feel like a community-run zine or a chaotic investigation board.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Tactile "paper" surfaces and textures.
- Thick, high-contrast borders (Soft Brutalism).
- Playful, comic-style interaction patterns.
- Warm, appetizing, and culturally resonant color palette.

### Named Rules
**The Soft Brutalism Rule.** Every card and interactive element must have a visible border (≥2px) in Investigation Ink Black. Shadow is avoided in favor of offset "drop shadows" with solid colors.

- **Label** (Semi-bold, Small): Used for "Egg smell only" style badges and metrics.

## 4. Elevation

Depth is conveyed through tonal layering and offset solid shadows, rather than soft blurs.

### Named Rules
**The Paper Layering Rule.** Surfaces should overlap like physical paper scraps on an investigation board. Use slight rotations (1-2 degrees) on cards to enhance the "hand-pinned" feel.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use thick borders (2px+) for all primary UI containers.
- **Do** use "hand-drawn" or organic shapes for badges and labels.
- **Do** include local Kerala bakery slang in the UI copy (e.g., "Legendary Puff").
- **Do** use cream textures instead of flat white backgrounds.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use generic corporate SaaS "clean" styling.
- **Don't** use soft, blurred shadows or glassmorphism.
- **Don't** use "safe" corporate blue or navy palettes.
- **Don't** use perfectly aligned, sterile grids; allow for slight "organic" imperfections.

# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students, bakery lovers, food explorers, and Kerala meme culture communities. They are snack enthusiasts who value authentic local experiences and have a high bar for "mutta puff" quality. They often use the app while on the move (hyperlocal discovery).

## Product Purpose
MuttaMeter is a community-powered investigation platform for Kerala bakery culture. It aims to turn ordinary bakery visits into interactive social experiences by allowing users to rate puffs, report "scams" (no egg), and discover the best local spots through a fun, comic-inspired interface.

## Brand Personality
Chaotic, funny, and internet-native. It blends the seriousness of a "mystery investigation" with the playfulness of meme culture. Handcrafted, expressive, and colorful.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic corporate SaaS styling, minimalist "clean" UI, or sterile food-delivery app templates. No flat, emotionless grids or "safe" navy/blue palettes.

## Design Principles
1. **The Investigation is the Game**: Every review and photo upload should feel like adding a piece of evidence to a community investigation board.
2. **Culturally Authentic Chaos**: Embrace the "internet-native" and "meme culture" vibes of Kerala. Use humor and local slang (e.g., "Egg smell only") as a primary interface language.
3. **Tactile & Handcrafted**: Surfaces should feel physical—like cream paper textures, comic-style badges, and thick playful borders—distancing the app from "software" and closer to "community zine".
4. **Scam-Proof Transparency**: The "scam alert" system should be visually distinct and high-signal, making it the most important community utility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard Web Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA). Mobile-first focus ensures responsiveness for users visiting bakeries in person.
